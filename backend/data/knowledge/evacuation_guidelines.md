# Evacuation Guidelines

## Priority Criteria
1. **Critical Medical Needs**: Hospitals in flood zones, patients requiring life support.
2. **Vulnerable Population**: Elderly, children under 5, disabled individuals (typically ~18% of the population).
3. **High-Density Low-Elevation Areas**: Areas with high population density and rapid water accumulation.

## Handling Vulnerable Populations
Specialized transport must be arranged. Medical personnel must accompany critical patients. Shelters receiving vulnerable individuals must have adequate medical supplies and accessible facilities.

## Route Planning
- Identify designated "Evacuation Routes" which are typically elevated major arterial roads (e.g., Mount Road, GST Road).
- If an evacuation route is reported flooded (>0.5m), reroute immediately using secondary roads.
- Use live traffic and water level sensor data to dynamically update safe paths.

## Assembly Points
Designated high-ground areas within each zone serve as temporary assembly points before transport to larger shelters. Ensure assembly points have basic first aid and drinking water.
