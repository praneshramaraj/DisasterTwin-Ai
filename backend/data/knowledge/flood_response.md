# Flood Emergency Response Protocols

## Water Level Danger Thresholds
- **Normal (0.0 - 0.5m)**: Regular monitoring, check drainage systems.
- **Alert (0.5 - 1.5m)**: Activate local teams, standby emergency vehicles, prepare for localized flooding.
- **Danger (1.5 - 2.5m)**: Issue evacuation warnings, mobilize NDRF and SDRF, open primary shelters.
- **Extreme (>2.5m)**: Mandatory immediate evacuation, helicopter rescue for stranded individuals, full state machinery activation.

## Classification of Zones
Zones are classified based on elevation, proximity to water bodies, and historical flood data. High-risk zones like Velachery require preemptive resource allocation compared to higher elevation zones like Tambaram.

## Immediate Response Actions
1. Deploy rescue boats to areas reporting water levels above 1.5m.
2. Disconnect power supply in heavily flooded sectors to prevent electrocution.
3. Establish communication nodes at high elevation points.
