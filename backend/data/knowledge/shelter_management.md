# Shelter Management

## Activation Triggers
Shelters should be activated when the Risk Engine predicts a 'HIGH' or 'CRITICAL' risk level for a zone, or when water levels exceed 1.0m in residential areas.

## Capacity Management
- Monitor real-time occupancy. Do not exceed 110% of stated capacity to maintain hygiene and safety.
- If a shelter reaches 90% capacity, preemptively activate the nearest secondary shelter.
- Allocate 20% of capacity specifically for vulnerable populations and medical needs.

## Supply Requirements
- Minimum 3 days of supplies (food, water, basic medicine) must be present upon activation.
- Daily resupply runs using heavy-duty trucks or boats depending on road conditions.
- Critical supplies: clean drinking water (4L per person/day), non-perishable food, ORS, sanitation kits.

## Medical Provisions
Each shelter over 500 capacity must have at least two trained medical professionals on-site and a dedicated medical isolation area to prevent the spread of waterborne diseases.
